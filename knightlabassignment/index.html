Juan Soto's career trajectory

By Ashna Balroop

April 4, 2026

Juan Soto is a Major League Baseball player and outfielder for the New York Mets. Soto is known for his high walk rates, immense power, and elite plate discipline. He is also famous for the Soto Shuffle routine, where he moves his hips in the batter’s box after taking a ball, staring down pitchers. Currently, he is the linchpin of the Mets’ lineup. Soto was born in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. He grew up in a tough neighborhood and his mother limited the amount of time he spent outside. This led him to crumple up makeshift pieces of paper into baseballs to play indoors, according to Britannica. His dad also threw batting practice for him and took him to his men’s softball league games. The Washington Nationals signed Soto when he was 16 years old. He began his major league career in 2018 at the age of 19. Since then, he has played for four organizations: the Washington Nationals, the San Diego Padres, the New York Yankees and currently the Mets.
